# Roadmap

CDPF is intentionally small in Draft 0.1. The roadmap is directional rather than a promise.

- **0.1 — Core document package:** manifest, ordered blocks, coding primitives, medication and
  examination guidance, attachment hashing, extensions.
- **0.2 — Clinical object profiles:** richer ECG/image/drawing/report references and document-to-
  document relationships.
- **0.3 — Presentation profiles:** portable page/layout hints without making presentation normative.
- **0.4 — Provenance and amendments:** authorship chains, amendments, supersession, and audit events.
- **0.5 — Signatures and integrity:** detached cryptographic signatures and canonicalization rules.
- **Pre-1.0 interoperability work:** publish non-normative mappings to FHIR resources and other
  established standards where useful.

Encryption at rest and transport security are expected to be provided by the host application or
transport until a dedicated encrypted-package profile is designed.
